Privacy & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026 · Effective immediately
Core Privacy Architecture: PDF Merger operates entirely client-side inside your web browser. When you select, convert, compress, or merge files, your documents are processed strictly in your local device memory. No files, document contents, or personal files are ever uploaded, transmitted to, or stored on any server.
Section 01

Client-Side File Processing & Data Security

Traditional online file utilities transfer your private files to remote cloud servers to process them. PDF Merger is designed with an opposite, privacy-first architecture:

  • Local Execution: All document parsing, rendering, text rasterization, and PDF/DOCX compilation are executed locally on your machine using modern web standards (HTML5 Web APIs, Canvas, Web Workers, and WebAssembly).
  • Zero Server Uploads: Your documents (PDFs, Word .docx/.doc files, images) never leave your device. Our web servers never receive, inspect, or retain your documents.
  • Session Isolation: Once you close the browser tab or refresh the page, all file data held in volatile browser memory is immediately purged by your browser's garbage collector.

Section 03

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your web browser.

  • First-Party Cookies: PDF Merger does not set first-party tracking cookies, user profiling cookies, or persistent authentication cookies.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Third-party partners (primarily Google for AdSense) may set cookies or web beacons to serve ads and evaluate traffic metrics in accordance with their privacy policies.

You can instruct your browser, through its settings, to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. Most modern browsers also support private browsing (Incognito) modes that automatically delete cookies when the session ends.

Section 04

Log Files and Infrastructure Data

Like virtually all websites hosted on modern web infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages or content delivery networks), basic web server log files are generated when you request web pages. These logs may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (often masked or aggregated);
  • Browser type, operating system, and language preferences;
  • Referring URLs and timestamps of page requests.

This data is used solely for technical site delivery, bandwidth monitoring, DDoS defense, and ensuring system stability. It is never combined with document contents or personal identities.

Section 05

Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) & Open-Source Libraries

To deliver fast load times, this site loads open-source JavaScript libraries (such as PDF-lib, PDF.js, Mammoth, and JSZip) and web typography through public Content Delivery Networks (including jsDelivr and Google Fonts).

When your browser fetches these static script files, the CDN servers receive your standard HTTP request headers (IP address and user agent). These CDNs operate under their respective privacy policies and serve only static scripts—they never receive or process your local document files.

Section 06

Children's Online Privacy Protection

PDF Merger is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Section 07

Your Rights & Data Subject Requests

Because we do not store, catalog, or transmit your files, personal identification, or accounts on our servers:

  • There is no central database containing your files or documents;

  • There is no personal profile for us to delete upon request, as we never collect one.

You maintain complete ownership and unilateral control over your files at all times on your local machine.
